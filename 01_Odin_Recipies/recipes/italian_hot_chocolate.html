Thick Italian Hot Chocolate (Cioccolata Calda)

Ingredients

  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 2 oz high-quality dark chocolate (60–70% cocoa), finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 tbsp granulated sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 1 tsp cornstarch (the secret to the signature thick texture)

Method

  1. Create the paste: In a small bowl, whisk the cornstarch and cocoa powder with 2 tablespoons of the milk until a smooth paste forms with no lumps.
  2. Heat the base: Pour the remaining milk and sugar into a small saucepan and bring to a low simmer over medium heat.
  3. Thicken: Whisk the cocoa-cornstarch paste into the warm milk. Continuous whisking prevents clumping.
  4. Melt and finish: Reduce the heat to low, add the chopped dark chocolate, and stir constantly.
  5. Keep stirring until the chocolate is completely melted and the mixture thickens to a glossy, coat-the-back-of-a-spoon consistency (about 2–3 minutes).
  6. Pour into a mug and serve immediately.
