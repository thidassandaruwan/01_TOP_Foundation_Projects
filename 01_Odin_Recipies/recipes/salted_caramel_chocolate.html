Spiced Mexican Iced Chocolate

Ingredients

  • 1 cup milk (any variety)
  • 2 tbsp chocolate syrup
  • 1 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp caramel sauce (plus extra for drizzling)
  • A generous pinch of flaky sea salt
  • 1.5 cups ice cubes
  • Whipped cream for serving

Method

  1. Prep the glass: Drizzle a little caramel sauce along the inside walls of your serving glass and pop it in the freezer while you blend the drink.
  2. Blend: In a high-speed blender, combine the milk, chocolate syrup, cocoa powder, caramel sauce, sea salt, and ice.
  3. Process: Blend on high until perfectly smooth and frosty, ensuring no large ice chunks remain.
  4. Assemble: Pour the frozen chocolate into your chilled, caramel-swirled glass.
  5. Top with a mountain of whipped cream, an extra drizzle of caramel, and a tiny pinch of flaky sea salt.
