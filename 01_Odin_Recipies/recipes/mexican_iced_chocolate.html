Spiced Mexican Iced Chocolate

Ingredients

  • 1 cup almond milk (or milk of choice)
  • 2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1.5 tbsp pure maple syrup or honey
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • A tiny pinch of cayenne pepper (just enough for a subtle warmth!)
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • Ice Cubes

Method

  1. Dissolve the spices: In a small saucepan, combine 1/4 cup of the milk with the cocoa powder, cinnamon, cayenne pepper, and maple syrup.
  2. Heat gently over low heat, whisking until the cocoa is completely dissolved and smooth. (Heating a small amount first prevents the spices from clumping in cold liquid).
  3. Chill it down: Remove from heat and stir in the remaining cold milk and vanilla extract.
  4. Serve: Fill a tall glass to the brim with ice cubes and pour the spiced chocolate right over the top.
  5. Stir well and serve.
